Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

Interest earned on your original amount and on the interest already added, so growth speeds up over time instead of staying flat.

What is the Rule of 72?

A shortcut: divide 72 by the annual rate to estimate the years needed for money to double. At 8%, that is about 9 years.

Does compounding frequency matter?

A little. Daily compounding beats monthly, but the rate and the time matter far more than the frequency.

Are the returns shown guaranteed?

No. The calculator shows what happens at the rate you enter. Real investment returns vary, and savings rates change over time.

What is the difference between compound and simple interest?

Simple interest pays only on the original amount. Compound interest pays on the growing balance, so the gap between them widens every year.

How do monthly contributions help?

Each contribution gets its own time to grow, and the earliest ones grow the most. Regular contributions are the engine of most long-term plans.
